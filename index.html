Why we started this studio 3 months ago

When we founded yoursecondhome.co in July 2026, we set out to build the exact opposite of the traditional software agency.

When you pour your life into running a company, your workplace is your second home. We treat your operations with the exact same respect.

Most traditional companies don't need a Silicon Valley "moonshot." You don't need an overcomplicated enterprise system that requires three months of mandatory staff seminars just to figure out how to submit an invoice.
